# 📊 Retail Business Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Retail Business Analytics Dashboard** built using Power BI to analyze and visualize retail business performance. This project provides insights into sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and geographical trends through interactive dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs).

## 📸 Dashboard Preview

### 1. Sales Analysis Dashboard

Provides an overview of sales performance across product categories, customer segments, products, and countries.

**Key Metrics & Visualizations:**
- Total Sales
- Previous Year Sales
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Sales Growth
- Average Sales per Order
- Total Sales by Category
- Total Sales by Customer Segment
- Top 5 Selling Products by Year
- Monthly Sales Comparison
- Top 10 Countries by Sales


### 2. Profit Analysis Dashboard

Focuses on business profitability and identifies the most profitable products, categories, and customer segments.

**Key Metrics & Visualizations:**
- Total Profit
- Previous Year Profit
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Profit Growth
- Average Profit per Order
- Total Profit by Category
- Total Profit by Customer Segment
- Top 5 Profitable Products
- Monthly Profit Comparison

### 3. Customer Demographics Dashboard

Analyzes customer activity, customer value, order priorities, and geographical business performance.

**Key Metrics & Visualizations:**
- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Total Products
- Average Sales per State
- Top 10 Active Buyers
- Top 10 Valuable Customers
- Top 7 Profitable States
- Profit by Order Priority
- Sales and Profit by Region



## ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Navigation:** Three dedicated dashboard views for Sales, Profit, and Demographics.
- **Dynamic Filtering:** Year, Month, and Country filters.
- **KPI Tracking:** Visual representation of important business metrics.
- **Trend Analysis:** Monthly and year-over-year performance comparisons.
- **Product Performance:** Identification of top-selling and profitable products.
- **Customer Insights:** Analysis of active and valuable customers.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Comparison of sales and profitability across countries, states, and regions.
- **Data Visualization:** Bar charts, line charts, donut charts, and treemaps.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Data Visualization:** Power BI
- **Data Analysis:** DAX

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Develop an interactive retail analytics dashboard.
- Track key sales and profitability indicators.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Identify high-performing products and geographical regions.
- Transform business data into meaningful visual insights.
- Support data-driven business decision-making.

## 💡 Business Insights

This dashboard helps businesses:
- Identify the best-performing product categories.
- Monitor revenue and profit trends over time.
- Understand customer segments contributing to business performance.
- Identify the most valuable and active customers.
- Discover countries and states generating the highest sales.
- Analyze regional profitability patterns.
- Understand the impact of order priority on profitability.
